NeonType Race – Fast Online Multiplayer Typing Competition

Welcome to NeonType Race, the premier free real-time multiplayer typing race game. Test your typing speed in words per minute (WPM), improve your keyboard accuracy, and compete against friends or AI bots on a cyberpunk neon racing track. No downloads, installations, or account signups required.

How to Play NeonType Race

Create a private room with a 4-letter room code or join an existing race with friends. Choose your difficulty level: Easy paragraphs for beginners and warm-ups, Medium passages for casual racers, or Hard texts featuring technical and scientific vocabulary. As the countdown ends, type the highlighted text in real time. Watch your car speed down the neon track as you out-type your opponents.

Key Features

  • Real-time Multiplayer: Race head-to-head with up to 4 players using ultra-low latency WebSockets.
  • Difficulty Levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard paragraph pools tailored for all skill levels.
  • AI Bot Racers: Practice solo with dynamic AI bots ranging from 38 to 82 WPM.
  • Character-Level Heat Maps: Deep-dive into your typing rhythm, pinpointing tricky keys and keystroke latencies.
  • Spectator Mode: Watch live races in progress without interfering with racers.
  • Personal Best Ghosts: Race against your previous best typing runs to constantly improve.
  • Mobile & Responsive: Smooth glassmorphism UI optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

The Best Free Typing Game Alternative to TypeRacer and TypeRush

NeonType Race brings competitive touch typing into the modern web era. Designed with sleek neon visuals, instantaneous loading, strict data privacy, and advanced typing diagnostics, it offers a superior typing experience compared to legacy platforms like TypeRacer, Type Rush, and Nitro Type.
